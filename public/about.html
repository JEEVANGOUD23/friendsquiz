About FriendMatch

A free, simple way to find out who knows you best.

FriendMatch is a free online friendship quiz. The idea is simple: you answer 15 questions about yourself, such as your favourite food, your dream destination and what makes you happiest. You then share a personal link with your friends, and they try to guess what you picked. At the end, they see how many answers they got right.

How it works

  1. Create your quiz. Enter your name and choose the answers that describe you best.
  2. Share your link. Copy it or send it straight to your friends on WhatsApp.
  3. See the results. Your friends answer the questions and get a score, along with a message about how well they know you.

Why we built it

Good friendships are built on small details: the things you notice, remember and ask about. FriendMatch started as a way to make those details into a game. It is not meant to be serious or to rank your friends. It is meant to make you laugh, start a conversation and maybe show you something new about someone you care about.

Your privacy

You do not need to create an account or give us an email address to use FriendMatch. Your quiz answers are stored in the link you share, not in a database. You can read more on our Privacy Policy page.

Who is behind it

FriendMatch is an independent project created and maintained by Jeevan. It is free to use and supported by advertising. If you have a question, suggestion or bug report, please get in touch. We read every message.
