Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information FriendMatch ("we", "our", "the site") collects when you visit bestiequizg1.com and how it is used. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information you enter

When you create a quiz, you type your name and choose answers to the quiz questions. When a friend plays a quiz, they type their name. FriendMatch does not have user accounts and does not store this information in a database on our servers. Your name and answers are encoded in the quiz link itself. Anyone who has your link can see the creator's name and play the quiz, so only share it with people you trust.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, browser type, pages visited and timestamps. We use this information only to keep the site running securely and to understand overall traffic.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements. Google and its partners are third-party vendors that use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. These cookies allow Google and its partners to show you ads that may be relevant to your interests.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies by visiting aboutads.info. For more on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google's policy page.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings, including deleting existing cookies and blocking new ones. If you block cookies, some features of the site may not work as expected.

Sharing quiz links

When you choose to share a quiz on WhatsApp, the site opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message and your quiz link. What happens next is governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy. We do not receive any information about who you share with.

Third-party links

Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to read their policies.

Children's privacy

FriendMatch is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the site, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, such as the right to ask what we hold, to correct it or to have it deleted. Because we do not keep your quiz data on our servers, there is usually nothing for us to delete, but you can contact us with any request and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page. Please check back occasionally.
