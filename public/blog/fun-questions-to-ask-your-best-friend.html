30 Fun Questions to Ask Your Best Friend

By the FriendMatch team · Updated October 2026

Running out of things to talk about is rare between close friends, but sometimes the conversation stays on the same few topics. A good question can open a whole new side of someone you thought you knew completely. Here are 30 questions, grouped by mood, to use on road trips, at sleepovers, or any time you want to talk.

Light and silly

  1. If you could only eat one food for the rest of your life, what would it be?
  2. What is the most embarrassing song on your playlist?
  3. Which cartoon character do you think you are most like?
  4. If you won a free holiday for two, where would we go?
  5. What is a skill you wish you could learn instantly?
  6. What is the strangest dream you can remember?

Memories

  1. What is your favourite memory of the two of us?
  2. What was your first impression of me?
  3. What is the funniest thing that has happened to us?
  4. Which trip or day out would you repeat tomorrow?
  5. What is something you remember from childhood that still makes you smile?

Dreams and plans

  1. Where do you see yourself in five years?
  2. If money was not a concern, how would you spend your days?
  3. What is one thing you want to achieve this year?
  4. Which place in the world do you most want to visit?
  5. What would you like to be known for?

Would you rather

  1. Would you rather travel to the past or the future?
  2. Would you rather be able to fly or be invisible?
  3. Would you rather live by the sea or in the mountains?
  4. Would you rather have more time or more money?
  5. Would you rather always be early or always be late?

Deeper questions

  1. What makes you feel most appreciated?
  2. What is something you are proud of that you rarely talk about?
  3. What do you think makes a friendship last?
  4. Is there something you wish people understood about you?
  5. When do you feel most like yourself?

For the two of us

  1. What is one thing I do that always makes you laugh?
  2. What is something you would like us to do together that we have never done?
  3. If our friendship was a film, what would it be called?
  4. What is the best advice I have ever given you?

How to get the most from these questions

Do not rush. Ask a question, listen to the whole answer, then ask a follow-up before moving on. The best conversations usually come from the follow-up, not from the original question. And answer them yourself too, because friendships grow when both people share.
